---
title: Climate change
description: Climate change is the long-term shift in global temperatures and weather patterns, now driven mainly by greenhouse gases from human activity.
---

**Climate change** means a lasting change in the average state of the climate. Earth's climate has varied naturally through ice ages and warm periods, but since the 19th century the main driver has been human activity, chiefly the burning of coal, oil and gas, together with deforestation and farming.

## The greenhouse effect

Gases such as carbon dioxide, methane and nitrous oxide trap heat in the lower atmosphere, a natural process called the **greenhouse effect** that keeps the planet habitable. Adding more of these gases strengthens the effect. Carbon dioxide levels have risen from about 280 parts per million before the industrial era to over 420 today.[^1] The global average surface temperature has risen by about 1.1 to 1.3 degrees Celsius compared with the late 19th century.

## Observed effects

- **Melting ice**: [glaciers](../../landforms/glaciers/) and the ice sheets of Greenland and Antarctica are losing mass, and Arctic sea ice is shrinking.
- **Sea level rise**: caused by melting ice and the expansion of warming water; it threatens [coastlines](../../landforms/coastlines/), [deltas](../../water/deltas/) and low islands such as those of [Fiji](../../../world/oceania/fiji/).
- **Extreme weather**: more frequent heatwaves, heavier downpours and, in some regions, more intense [tropical cyclones](../tropical-cyclones/).
- **Ecosystems**: warming and ocean acidification harm [coral reefs](../../biomes/coral-reefs/), and the zones of the [tundra](../../biomes/tundra/) and [taiga](../../biomes/taiga/) are shifting.
- **Water resources**: shrinking mountain ice and altered rainfall affect rivers and the supply of fresh water.

## Responses

Responses fall into two groups. **Mitigation** reduces emissions through renewable energy, efficiency and protecting forests. **Adaptation** prepares for impacts, for example with sea walls, drought-resistant crops and early warning systems. The 2015 Paris Agreement aims to hold warming well below 2 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels.

:::caution
Effects are uneven. Low-lying and dry regions, and countries with fewer resources, tend to face the greatest risks, even though they have contributed least to emissions.
:::
