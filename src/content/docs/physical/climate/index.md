Climate describes the typical pattern of weather in a place over decades. It is shaped by latitude, altitude, the distance from the sea, the layout of mountains and the movement of air and water around the planet. These pages explain how those factors combine to give the deserts, rainforests, ice sheets and temperate lands that the rest of this wiki describes.

The section begins with the basic distinction between weather and climate, then looks at the main climate classification used by geographers. It continues with large seasonal and episodic systems, such as monsoons and tropical cyclones, and with the ocean's role in moving heat. The final page covers how human activity is changing the climate.

Climate also determines which plants and animals can live where, so this section is closely tied to the [biomes](../biomes/). It also draws on the [water cycle](../water/water-cycle/), the [oceans](../water/oceans/) and landforms such as [mountains](../landforms/mountains/) and [deserts](../landforms/deserts/). For unfamiliar terms, see the [glossary](../../reference/glossary/).
