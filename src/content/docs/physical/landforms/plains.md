A **plain** is a broad area of land with little change in elevation, either flat or gently rolling. Plains cover about a third of the land surface and are often built from sediment laid down by rivers, glaciers or ancient seas. Their deep, even soils make many plains among the world's best farmland, and they support much of the world's population. Examples include the Great Plains of North America, the North European Plain and the Indo-Gangetic Plain. Plains are often covered by [grasslands](../../biomes/grasslands/).
