A **cave** is a natural opening in rock, big enough for a person to enter. Most are **solution caves**, formed when slightly acidic rainwater seeps through limestone and slowly dissolves it. Others are carved by waves in sea cliffs, formed by flowing lava, or hollowed out within [glaciers](../glaciers/). Dripping mineral-rich water builds stalactites and stalagmites. Caves link to underground rivers and [groundwater](../../water/groundwater/), and some shelter unusual animals and ancient human remains.
