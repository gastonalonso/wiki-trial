The **Mediterranean biome** is a shrubland of drought-resistant evergreen plants, found where winters are mild and wet and summers are hot and dry. Besides the lands around the Mediterranean Sea, it occurs in California, central Chile, the Cape region of South Africa and southwestern and southern Australia. Typical plants include olive, cork oak, rosemary and lavender, and fire is a regular feature. The region is a hotspot of plant diversity.
