A **biome** is a large community of plants and animals adapted to a particular climate. Biomes are usually named for their dominant vegetation, such as forest, grassland or tundra, and they cover broad regions that often span several continents. Temperature and rainfall are the main controls, so the biomes follow the [climate zones](../climate/climate-zones/) quite closely.

Biomes are also shaped by soil, fire, grazing animals and altitude. Marine and coastal biomes, such as coral reefs and mangroves, are defined by water temperature, salinity and tides rather than by rainfall. Human activity has reduced or altered many biomes, through farming, logging, urban growth and [climate change](../climate/climate-change/).

## Summary of biomes

| Biome | Climate | Typical vegetation | Example region |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tropical rainforest | Hot and wet all year | Dense, layered evergreen forest | Amazon, Congo basin |
| Savanna | Warm, with wet and dry seasons | Grass with scattered trees | East Africa |
| Temperate forest | Moderate, four seasons | Broadleaf and mixed forest | Eastern North America, Europe |
| Taiga | Long cold winters, short summers | Conifer forest | Canada, Siberia |
| Tundra | Very cold, low precipitation | Mosses, lichens, low shrubs | Arctic coasts |
| Grasslands | Semi-arid to moderate | Grasses, few trees | Prairies, steppes, pampas |
| Mediterranean | Mild wet winters, hot dry summers | Evergreen shrubs and small trees | Southern Europe, California |
| Coral reefs | Warm, clear, shallow seas | Coral and algae | Coral Triangle, Caribbean |
| Mangroves | Tropical tidal coasts | Salt-tolerant trees | Sundarbans, Southeast Asia |

## Pages in this section

- [Tropical rainforest](tropical-rainforest/): the most biodiverse land biome, found near the equator.
- [Savanna](savanna/): tropical grassland with scattered trees and great herds of grazers.
- [Temperate forests](temperate-forests/): deciduous and mixed forests of the mid-latitudes.
- [Taiga](taiga/): the vast northern conifer forest, also called boreal forest.
- [Tundra](tundra/): the cold, treeless lands of the Arctic and high mountains.
- [Grasslands](grasslands/): prairies, steppes and pampas of the continental interiors.
- [Mediterranean](mediterranean/): shrubland with dry summers and mild wet winters.
- [Coral reefs](coral-reefs/): rich marine ecosystems built by tiny animals.
- [Mangroves](mangroves/): salt-tolerant forests on tropical shorelines.

For famous examples, see the [Amazon rainforest](../../places/amazon-rainforest/) and the [Great Barrier Reef](../../places/great-barrier-reef/).
