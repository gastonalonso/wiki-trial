**Mangroves** are trees and shrubs that grow in the intertidal zone of tropical and subtropical coasts, where their tangled roots stand in salt water. They filter salt, take in oxygen through special root structures and trap sediment. Mangrove forests shelter young fish, shield shorelines from storms and store large amounts of carbon. The Sundarbans, shared by India and Bangladesh, is the largest mangrove forest. They are often cleared for shrimp farming and building.
