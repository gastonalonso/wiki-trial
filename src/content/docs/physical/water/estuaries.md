An **estuary** is a partly enclosed coastal body of water where a river meets the sea and fresh water mixes with salt water. The water is brackish, and its salinity changes with the tides and the season. Estuaries are rich nurseries for fish and shellfish and are bordered by mudflats and salt marshes. Many large ports lie on them. They differ from [deltas](../deltas/), which build outward into the sea, because tides here are strong enough to carry sediment away.
