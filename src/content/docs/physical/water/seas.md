A **sea** is a large body of salt water, smaller than an ocean and usually partly enclosed by land. Some, such as the Mediterranean and the Caribbean, are connected to an ocean by narrow straits. Others, such as the Caspian Sea, are enclosed by land and are really large salt lakes. Seas are typically shallower than the open ocean and are shaped by nearby coasts, rivers and climate. They are major routes for trade and fishing, and the Mediterranean lies between [Europe](../../../world/europe/) and [Africa](../../../world/africa/).
