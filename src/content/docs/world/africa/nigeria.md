**Nigeria** is in West Africa on the Gulf of Guinea. It is the most populous country in Africa and one of the most linguistically diverse in the world, with several hundred languages spoken.[^1] Its cities, including Lagos, are among the fastest-growing on the continent.

## Geography

The Niger and Benue [rivers](../../../physical/water/rivers/) meet in central Nigeria and flow south into the Niger [Delta](../../../physical/water/deltas/), a vast network of creeks and [mangroves](../../../physical/biomes/mangroves/) on the coast. Behind the coastal belt, a lowland forest zone gives way to savanna on the central plateau and to the dry Sahel in the north. The highest ground is in the eastern highlands near the Cameroon border.

## Climate

The south has a humid tropical climate with heavy rain, partly [tropical rainforest](../../../physical/biomes/tropical-rainforest/). Rainfall decreases northward, and the north has a long dry season, with dusty Harmattan winds from the Sahara between December and February.

## Regions and places

- **Lagos**, the largest city and commercial centre.
- **Abuja**, the planned capital, in the centre.
- **The Niger Delta**, a major wetland and oil-producing region.
- **The northern savanna**, with historic cities such as Kano.
