**Morocco** occupies the north-western corner of Africa, facing Spain across the Strait of Gibraltar. It is the only African country with both Atlantic and Mediterranean coastlines, and its landscape ranges from fertile coastal plains to high mountains and desert.

## Geography

The Atlas Mountains cross the country from south-west to north-east, in several ranges including the High Atlas, where Toubkal stands. North of them lie the fertile Atlantic plains where most people live. South and east of the mountains, the land falls away to the pre-Saharan steppe and the [Sahara](../../../places/sahara/), with dunes near Merzouga. The long [coastline](../../../physical/landforms/coastlines/) includes the Atlantic ports of Casablanca and Rabat.

## Climate

The north and the coast have a [Mediterranean](../../../physical/biomes/mediterranean/) climate with mild, wet winters and hot, dry summers. The mountains get snow in winter, and the south is arid. The cold Canary Current moderates temperatures along the Atlantic coast (see [ocean currents](../../../physical/climate/ocean-currents/)).

## Regions and places

- **Casablanca**, the largest city and main port.
- **Marrakesh and Fez**, historic imperial cities.
- **The Atlas Mountains**, home to Amazigh villages and skiing.
- **The desert south**, with oases and caravan routes.
