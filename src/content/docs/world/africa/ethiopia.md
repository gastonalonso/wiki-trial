**Ethiopia** is a landlocked country in the Horn of Africa and one of the continent's most mountainous. Much of it is a high plateau, often called the roof of Africa, split down the middle by the East African Rift. It has an ancient state tradition and its own script.

## Geography

The Ethiopian Highlands are a mass of [mountains](../../../physical/landforms/mountains/) and [plateaus](../../../physical/landforms/plateaus/) cut by deep gorges. Lake Tana in the north-west is the source of the Blue Nile, which contributes most of the water of the [Nile](../../../places/nile/) downstream. The Rift Valley crosses the centre of the country and holds a chain of lakes, while in the north-east the Danakil Depression is among the hottest and lowest places on Earth, with active [volcanoes](../../../physical/landforms/volcanoes/).

## Climate

Climate depends on altitude. The lowlands are hot and dry, the middle slopes are temperate and the high plateau is cool. Most rain falls in a summer wet season linked to the [monsoons](../../../physical/climate/monsoons/), and failures of these rains have caused serious droughts.

## Regions and places

- **The northern highlands**, with historic churches and the Simien Mountains.
- **The Rift Valley lakes**, a string of lakes and wildlife reserves.
- **The Danakil Depression**, a desert of salt flats and volcanic activity.
- **Addis Ababa**, one of Africa's highest capitals, at about 2,400 m.
