Africa is the second-largest continent, covering about 30 million square kilometres, and is home to roughly 1.5 billion people. It straddles the equator, so its climates run from the tropical rainforests of the Congo Basin to the savannas of East Africa, the world's largest hot desert in the north, and a Mediterranean-type coast at both the northern and southern tips.

The continent is largely a high plateau. Its great rivers, including the [Nile](../../places/nile/), cross the plateau's edges in rapids and falls, among them [Victoria Falls](../../places/victoria-falls/). The East African Rift splits the east into long valleys and lakes, and carries volcanoes such as Kilimanjaro. In the north the [Sahara](../../places/sahara/) separates the Mediterranean world from sub-Saharan Africa.

Africa is also the continent with the youngest population and some of the fastest-growing cities. See [population](../../human/population/) and [urbanization](../../human/urbanization/) for the wider picture.

## Country pages

- [Egypt](egypt/): the Nile valley and delta, desert, and one of the oldest states.
- [Ethiopia](ethiopia/): a highland country with a long history and the source of the Blue Nile.
- [Kenya](kenya/): savanna, Mount Kenya and the Indian Ocean coast.
- [Morocco](morocco/): the Atlas Mountains and a gateway between Africa and Europe.
- [Nigeria](nigeria/): Africa's most populous country, on the Niger and Benue rivers.
- [South Africa](south-africa/): the southern tip, with plateaus, mountains and two oceans.
