**Egypt** lies mainly in north-eastern Africa, with the Sinai Peninsula extending into Asia. Almost all of its people live along the [Nile](../../../places/nile/) and in its delta, a green ribbon in a country that is otherwise desert. It is one of the oldest continuously settled regions on Earth.

## Geography

The Nile enters from the south and flows north for roughly 1,500 km inside Egypt, spreading into a broad [delta](../../../physical/water/deltas/) on the Mediterranean coast. West of the river lies the Libyan Desert, part of the [Sahara](../../../places/sahara/), and to the east the Eastern Desert rises towards the Red Sea hills. The Sinai Peninsula holds the country's highest mountains, while the Red Sea coast is known for [coral reefs](../../../physical/biomes/coral-reefs/).

## Climate

Egypt has a hot [desert climate](../../../physical/landforms/deserts/) with very little rain. Summers are hot and winters mild, and the Mediterranean coast receives most of what little rainfall there is. Because rain is so scarce, farming depends on Nile water, which is stored behind the Aswan High Dam.

:::tip[Did you know?]
About 95 percent of Egyptians live on roughly 5 percent of the land, along the Nile valley and delta.[^1]
:::

## Regions and places

- **Cairo and Giza**, the capital region, home to the pyramids.
- **Upper Egypt**, the southern valley, with Luxor and Aswan.
- **The Nile Delta**, an intensely farmed lowland around Alexandria.
- **Sinai**, a mountainous peninsula between two gulfs of the Red Sea.
