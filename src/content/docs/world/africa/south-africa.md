**South Africa** lies at the southern tip of the continent, with the Atlantic Ocean on the west and the Indian Ocean on the east. It is unusual in having three capital cities, each hosting one branch of government, and 12 official languages.

## Quick facts

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Capital | Pretoria (executive), Cape Town (legislative), Bloemfontein (judicial) |
| Population | about 63 million (2024) |
| Area | about 1,221,000 km² |
| Official language(s) | 12, including Zulu, Xhosa, Afrikaans and English |
| Currency | Rand |
| Highest point | Mafadi, about 3,451 m |

## Geography

Most of the interior is a high [plateau](../../../physical/landforms/plateaus/) called the Highveld, ringed by the Great Escarpment, whose eastern edge forms the Drakensberg [mountains](../../../physical/landforms/mountains/). To the west lies the arid Karoo and, further north, the Kalahari. The [coastline](../../../physical/landforms/coastlines/) is long and mostly straight, ending at Cape Agulhas, the southernmost point of Africa. The Orange River drains the interior westward to the Atlantic.

## Climate

The climate is mostly temperate and sunny. The south-western Cape has a [Mediterranean](../../../physical/biomes/mediterranean/) climate with winter rain and a distinctive shrubland, while the east is wetter and subtropical and the north-west is semi-desert. Two [ocean currents](../../../physical/climate/ocean-currents/), the warm Agulhas and the cold Benguela, shape the east and west coasts.

:::tip[Did you know?]
The Cape Floristic Region in the south-west is one of the smallest of the world's plant kingdoms but one of the richest in species.
:::

## Regions and places

- **Gauteng**, the economic heartland, around Johannesburg and Pretoria.
- **The Western Cape**, with Cape Town and Table Mountain.
- **KwaZulu-Natal**, a subtropical east coast province.
- **Kruger National Park**, a large [savanna](../../../physical/biomes/savanna/) reserve in the north-east.
