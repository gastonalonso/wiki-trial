**Japan** is an island nation in the north-western Pacific, off the east coast of Asia. It has about 14,000 islands, but four main islands, Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu and Shikoku, hold nearly all of the land and people. Mountains cover most of the country, so settlement is crowded onto small coastal plains.

## Geography

Japan lies on the Pacific Ring of Fire, where plates collide. The result is a long spine of [mountains](../../../physical/landforms/mountains/) with many [volcanoes](../../../physical/landforms/volcanoes/), such as Mount Fuji, and frequent earthquakes and tsunamis. The [coastline](../../../physical/landforms/coastlines/) is long and deeply indented, and the nearby Japan Trench is one of the deep ocean trenches (see also the [Mariana Trench](../../../places/mariana-trench/)).

## Climate

Climate runs from subarctic in Hokkaido to subtropical in the southern islands. Summers are hot and humid across most of the country and winters in the north-west bring very heavy snow, as cold air picks up moisture over the Sea of Japan. In late summer and autumn, typhoons (see [tropical cyclones](../../../physical/climate/tropical-cyclones/)) often hit the south and east.

:::tip[Did you know?]
Greater Tokyo, with more than 35 million residents, is among the largest urban areas in the world.[^1]
:::

## Regions and places

- **Kanto**, the plain around Tokyo and Yokohama.
- **Kansai**, with Osaka and Kyoto.
- **Hokkaido**, a cooler northern island with forests and volcanoes.
- **The southern islands**, including Okinawa.
