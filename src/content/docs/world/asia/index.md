Asia is the largest continent by both area and population, covering about 44.6 million square kilometres and holding roughly 60 percent of the world's people. It stretches from the Arctic coast of Siberia to the equatorial islands of Indonesia, and from the shores of the Mediterranean to the western Pacific.

Its physical extremes are well known. The Himalayas, including [Mount Everest](../../places/mount-everest/), are the highest mountains on Earth. [Lake Baikal](../../places/lake-baikal/) is the deepest lake, and the shores of the [Dead Sea](../../places/dead-sea/) are the lowest dry land. Great rivers such as the Yangtze, the Ganges and the Mekong rise on the Tibetan Plateau and water some of the most densely populated plains in the world.

Climate varies as widely. Seasonal [monsoons](../../physical/climate/monsoons/) bring most of the rain to South and Southeast Asia, while the interior of the continent includes cold steppe and desert, and the north is [taiga](../../physical/biomes/taiga/) and [tundra](../../physical/biomes/tundra/). Asia also has many of the world's largest cities (see [cities](../../human/cities/)).

## Country pages

- [China](china/): the most populous country in East Asia, from the Tibetan Plateau to the Pacific coast.
- [India](india/): a subcontinent bounded by the Himalayas and the Indian Ocean.
- [Indonesia](indonesia/): an archipelago of thousands of volcanic islands on the equator.
- [Japan](japan/): a mountainous island country on the Pacific Ring of Fire.
- [Mongolia](mongolia/): a landlocked country of steppe and the Gobi Desert.
- [Nepal](nepal/): a Himalayan country that holds eight of the world's ten highest peaks.
