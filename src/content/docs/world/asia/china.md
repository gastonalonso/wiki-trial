**China** occupies most of East Asia and is among the largest countries in the world by area. Its terrain steps down from the high plateaus and mountains of the west to broad plains and a long coast in the east, and most of its population lives in the eastern third.

## Geography

The Tibetan Plateau, the highest and largest [plateau](../../../physical/landforms/plateaus/) on Earth, lies in the south-west and is bordered by the Himalayas, including [Mount Everest](../../../places/mount-everest/). From it flow the Yangtze and the Yellow River, two of the longest [rivers](../../../physical/water/rivers/) in Asia, which cross the North China Plain and the south-eastern lowlands before reaching the Pacific. The north has the Gobi and Taklamakan [deserts](../../../physical/landforms/deserts/), and the far south has limestone karst scenery.

## Climate

China is large enough to have almost every climate type. The south-east is warm and humid and shaped by the East Asian [monsoon](../../../physical/climate/monsoons/), the north is cold in winter and dry, and the plateau is cold and thin-aired. Typhoons, a regional form of [tropical cyclone](../../../physical/climate/tropical-cyclones/), strike the south-east coast in late summer.

:::note
The Yellow River carries so much fine silt from the Loess Plateau that it was once known for repeatedly changing its course on the North China Plain.
:::

## Regions and places

- **The North China Plain**, a major farming and population centre.
- **The Yangtze delta**, around Shanghai, one of the world's largest urban areas.
- **Sichuan Basin**, a fertile, densely settled basin in the south-west.
- **Xinjiang and Inner Mongolia**, dry frontier regions to the north-west and north.
