**Australia** occupies the whole of the continent of the same name and the island of Tasmania. It is the sixth-largest country in the world by area, yet it is thinly populated, with most people living in cities along the south-east and east coasts. It is the flattest and, apart from Antarctica, the driest inhabited continent.

## Geography

Most of the continent is a low, ancient plateau worn down over hundreds of millions of years ([plateaus](../../../physical/landforms/plateaus/)). The Great Dividing Range runs along the east coast, and the interior, known as the outback, is mostly flat and dry. Rising from the plain in the Northern Territory is **Uluru**, a sandstone monolith that is sacred to the Anangu people; see [Uluru](../../../places/uluru/). The Murray–Darling system is the main river basin ([rivers](../../../physical/water/rivers/)), though many inland rivers flow only after rain.

The coastline is long and varied. Off Queensland lies the [Great Barrier Reef](../../../places/great-barrier-reef/), the largest coral reef system in the world, visible from space ([coral reefs](../../../physical/biomes/coral-reefs/)). Tasmania, to the south, is a mountainous island ([islands](../../../physical/landforms/islands/)).

## Climate and biomes

The centre and west are desert and semi-arid country, where the Great Victoria, Gibson and Simpson are among several large [deserts](../../../physical/landforms/deserts/). The north is tropical, with a wet and dry season and areas of [savanna](../../../physical/biomes/savanna/) and [tropical rainforest](../../../physical/biomes/tropical-rainforest/). The south-east and south-west have temperate and [Mediterranean](../../../physical/biomes/mediterranean/) climates. Droughts and bushfires are recurrent hazards, and rainfall is strongly affected by [El Niño](../../../physical/climate/el-nino/) cycles.

:::note[Name of a continent]
Australia is sometimes grouped with New Zealand and nearby islands under the name Australasia, and with the Pacific islands as [Oceania](../).
:::

## Regions

There are six states, Western Australia, South Australia, Queensland, New South Wales, Victoria and Tasmania, plus the Northern Territory and the Australian Capital Territory. Sydney and Melbourne are the largest cities ([cities](../../../human/cities/)). The nation lies close to [New Zealand](../new-zealand/) and sits within the Pacific region.
