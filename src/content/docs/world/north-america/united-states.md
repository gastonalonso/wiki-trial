The **United States** covers the central part of North America, plus Alaska in the northwest and the Hawaiian Islands in the Pacific. It is among the largest countries in the world by area and the third most populous, and it contains almost every major climate and landscape type.

## Geography

The East is dominated by the Atlantic coastal plain and the old, rounded **Appalachian Mountains**. West of them lie the interior plains, with the Great Lakes to the north and the Mississippi–Missouri river system draining the centre of the country to the Gulf of Mexico ([rivers](../../../physical/water/rivers/), [deltas](../../../physical/water/deltas/)). The Great Plains rise gently westward to the Rocky Mountains, beyond which the Basin and Range, the Sierra Nevada and the Cascades run down to the Pacific. See [plains](../../../physical/landforms/plains/) and [mountains](../../../physical/landforms/mountains/).

Erosion by the Colorado River has carved the [Grand Canyon](../../../places/grand-canyon/) through the plateaus of the Southwest, and the Hawaiian Islands are a chain of active [volcanoes](../../../physical/landforms/volcanoes/) over a hot spot.

## Climate and biomes

The country has humid subtropical conditions in the Southeast, continental climates in the North and Midwest, arid and semi-arid conditions across the Southwest, and Mediterranean-style summers and mild, wet winters in coastal California. See [Mediterranean](../../../physical/biomes/mediterranean/) and [temperate forests](../../../physical/biomes/temperate-forests/). Hurricanes threaten the Gulf and Atlantic coasts, and tornadoes are frequent in the central plains ([tropical cyclones](../../../physical/climate/tropical-cyclones/)).

:::caution
Hurricane season in the Atlantic runs from 1 June to 30 November, and the Gulf coast is among the most exposed shorelines in the world.
:::

## Regions

The census bureau groups the fifty states into four regions: Northeast, Midwest, South and West. The largest cities include New York City, Los Angeles and Chicago ([cities](../../../human/cities/), [urbanization](../../../human/urbanization/)). The country has long been a major destination for [migration](../../../human/migration/), and it shares land borders with Canada and Mexico ([borders](../../../human/borders/)).
