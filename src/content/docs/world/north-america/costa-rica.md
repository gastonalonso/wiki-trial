**Costa Rica** is a small country on the Central American isthmus, between Nicaragua and Panama. Despite covering only a fraction of one percent of the world's land, it holds a remarkable share of its plant and animal species, and it is known for protecting large areas of forest as national parks.

## Geography

A chain of mountains runs down the centre of the country, from the volcanic Cordillera de Guanacaste in the north to the Talamanca range in the south, where Cerro Chirripó rises. Several of the cordillera's peaks are active [volcanoes](../../../physical/landforms/volcanoes/), including Arenal and Poás. Most people live in the fertile Central Valley, a high basin with a mild climate that holds the capital.

Coastal plains lie on both sides: the humid Caribbean lowlands and the drier, more rugged Pacific coast with its peninsulas and gulfs. The country is narrow, so a traveller can cross from one ocean to the other in a few hours.

## Climate and biomes

Costa Rica lies in the tropics and has two main seasons, a dry season and a wet season, rather than a winter and summer. The Caribbean side receives rain nearly all year, while the northern Pacific side has a pronounced dry season. Altitude changes the picture: temperatures fall steadily on the mountain slopes.

The result is a mosaic of habitats, including [tropical rainforest](../../../physical/biomes/tropical-rainforest/), high-altitude cloud forest, dry forest and [mangroves](../../../physical/biomes/mangroves/) along the coasts. Offshore, parts of the Caribbean coast are fringed by [coral reefs](../../../physical/biomes/coral-reefs/).

:::tip[Did you know?]
Costa Rica abolished its standing army in 1948 and has directed part of that spending toward education and conservation.
:::

## Notable places

The cloud forests of Monteverde, the beaches of the Nicoya Peninsula and the turtle-nesting coast at Tortuguero are among the best-known sites. Frequent rain and steep terrain mean the country is also crossed by many short rivers and [waterfalls](../../../physical/water/waterfalls/).
