**Mexico** lies between the United States and Central America, with coasts on the Pacific Ocean, the Gulf of Mexico and the Caribbean Sea. It is the most populous Spanish-speaking country and one of the world's most biologically diverse, with landscapes ranging from desert to rainforest.

## Geography

The heart of the country is a high central plateau bordered by two mountain ranges, the Sierra Madre Occidental in the west and the Sierra Madre Oriental in the east. The southern edge of the plateau is the Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt, a line of [volcanoes](../../../physical/landforms/volcanoes/) that includes Pico de Orizaba, the highest peak, and Popocatépetl, which overlooks Mexico City. The capital itself stands on the bed of an old lake basin at more than 2,200 metres.

In the north the plateau gives way to the Chihuahuan and Sonoran [deserts](../../../physical/landforms/deserts/). The Yucatán Peninsula in the southeast is a flat limestone platform riddled with sinkholes known as cenotes and underground rivers, a good example of [cave](../../../physical/landforms/caves/) and [groundwater](../../../physical/water/groundwater/) systems. Baja California is a long, narrow peninsula separated from the mainland by the Gulf of California.

## Climate and biomes

Because the Tropic of Cancer crosses the country, climate depends strongly on altitude. The coastal lowlands are hot and humid, the highlands mild, and the north dry. [Tropical cyclones](../../../physical/climate/tropical-cyclones/) strike both coasts between roughly June and November. The south holds [tropical rainforest](../../../physical/biomes/tropical-rainforest/) in Chiapas and Oaxaca, while the Caribbean coast supports [mangroves](../../../physical/biomes/mangroves/) and part of the large Mesoamerican barrier reef.

:::note
Mexico recognises dozens of indigenous languages alongside Spanish, and several of them, such as Nahuatl and Maya, are spoken by more than a million people. See [languages](../../../human/languages/).
:::

## Notable places

Mexico City is one of the largest urban areas in the Americas ([cities](../../../human/cities/)). Archaeological sites such as Teotihuacán and Chichén Itzá recall the pre-Columbian civilisations of Mesoamerica, and the Copper Canyon in the Sierra Madre Occidental is a network of [canyons](../../../physical/landforms/canyons/) larger in total than the Grand Canyon.
