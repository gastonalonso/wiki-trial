Europe is the western part of the Eurasian landmass and the sixth-largest continent, covering about 10 million square kilometres. It is bounded by the Arctic Ocean to the north, the Atlantic to the west and the Mediterranean and Black seas to the south. The border with Asia is a convention, usually drawn along the Ural Mountains and the Caucasus.

Its coast is long and complex, with large peninsulas such as Scandinavia, Iberia, Italy and the Balkans, and many [islands](../../physical/landforms/islands/). Mountain chains, notably the Alps and the Pyrenees, lie in the south, while a broad lowland, the Great European Plain, reaches from France across Poland into Russia.

Most of Europe has a temperate climate warmed by the North Atlantic Drift, which gives mild winters at high latitudes (see [ocean currents](../../physical/climate/ocean-currents/)). The south has a [Mediterranean](../../physical/biomes/mediterranean/) climate, and the far north has [tundra](../../physical/biomes/tundra/). Europe is densely settled and highly urbanised, and its many [borders](../../human/borders/) reflect a long history.

## Country pages

- [Iceland](iceland/): a volcanic island in the North Atlantic.

- [Norway](norway/): a mountainous country of fjords on the Scandinavian peninsula.
- [Poland](poland/): a country of lowlands between the Baltic Sea and the Carpathians.
- [Spain](spain/): most of the Iberian Peninsula, with high plateaus and sunny coasts.
