**Spain** takes up about five-sixths of the Iberian Peninsula, between the Atlantic Ocean and the Mediterranean Sea, and also includes the Balearic and Canary islands and two small cities on the North African coast. It is one of the highest countries in Europe by average elevation.

## Geography

The core of the country is the Meseta, a large [plateau](../../../physical/landforms/plateaus/) at about 600 to 800 m, bordered by [mountains](../../../physical/landforms/mountains/): the Pyrenees in the north-east, the Cantabrian range along the north coast and the Sierra Nevada in the south. The Ebro, Tagus, Douro and Guadalquivir [rivers](../../../physical/water/rivers/) cross the plateau and the lowlands around it. Teide is a [volcano](../../../physical/landforms/volcanoes/) on Tenerife, and the south-east has some of Europe's driest, most [desert](../../../physical/landforms/deserts/)-like areas.

## Climate

Spain has a variety of climates. Most of the country is [Mediterranean](../../../physical/biomes/mediterranean/), with hot, dry summers. The north and north-west coast is cool and green, with year-round rain, the interior has hot summers and cold winters, and the Canary Islands are subtropical.

:::note
Southern Spain lies only about 14 km from Morocco across the Strait of Gibraltar, the narrowest crossing between Europe and Africa.
:::

## Regions and places

- **Madrid and the Meseta**, the dry central heartland.
- **Andalusia**, with the south coast and Sierra Nevada.
- **Catalonia**, around Barcelona on the north-east coast.
- **The Balearic and Canary Islands**, popular island groups.
