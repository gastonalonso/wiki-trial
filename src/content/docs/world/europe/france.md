**France** is a country in western Europe with a hexagonal outline, whose borders are set by the sea on three sides and by mountains and rivers on others. It also includes overseas regions, such as Guadeloupe and Réunion, and the figures below are for the whole country.[^1]

## Quick facts

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Capital | Paris |
| Population | about 68 million (2024) |
| Area | about 643,800 km² (about 551,500 km² in mainland France) |
| Official language(s) | French |
| Currency | Euro |
| Highest point | Mont Blanc, about 4,806 m, on the Italian border |

## Geography

Most of France is lowland or low hills. The Paris Basin and the Aquitaine Basin are drained by large [rivers](../../../physical/water/rivers/), the Seine, Loire, Garonne and Rhône. The Massif Central is an old, eroded upland in the centre. In the south-east, the Alps include Mont Blanc, and the Pyrenees mark the border with [Spain](../spain/). The Atlantic and Channel [coastlines](../../../physical/landforms/coastlines/) are rocky and tidal, while the Mediterranean coast is sunny and sheltered.

## Climate

Most of the country has an oceanic climate with mild, damp winters and warm summers, and it becomes more continental towards the east. The Mediterranean south has hot, dry summers and the Alps have a cold mountain climate. See [climate zones](../../../physical/climate/climate-zones/).

:::tip[Did you know?]
France has more time zones than any other country, because its overseas territories span the globe. See [time zones](../../../reference/time-zones/).
:::

## Regions and places

- **Île-de-France**, around Paris.
- **Normandy and Brittany**, with Atlantic and Channel coasts.
- **The Alps**, high [mountains](../../../physical/landforms/mountains/) and [glaciers](../../../physical/landforms/glaciers/).
- **Provence and the Riviera**, on the Mediterranean.
