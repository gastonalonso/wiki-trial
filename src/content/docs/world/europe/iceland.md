**Iceland** is an island country in the North Atlantic, just south of the Arctic Circle. It sits on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, where the North American and Eurasian plates pull apart, so it is among the most volcanically active places on Earth.

## Geography

The interior is a high, uninhabited plateau of lava, sand and ice, and nearly everyone lives around the coast. Roughly a tenth of the island is covered by [glaciers](../../../physical/landforms/glaciers/), such as Vatnajökull, the largest ice cap in Europe by volume, and beneath some of them lie [volcanoes](../../../physical/landforms/volcanoes/). Eruptions, geysers and hot springs are common, and geothermal heat supplies much of the country's heating. There are also many [waterfalls](../../../physical/water/waterfalls/) and a rugged [coastline](../../../physical/landforms/coastlines/) with fjords.

## Climate

The North Atlantic Drift keeps Iceland much milder than its latitude suggests, with cool summers and winters near freezing on the coast. The weather is changeable and windy. The highlands are colder and have [tundra](../../../physical/biomes/tundra/)-like vegetation.

:::note[Land of fire and ice]
Eruptions beneath glaciers can melt ice rapidly and cause sudden floods called jökulhlaups.
:::

## Regions and places

- **Reykjavík and the south-west**, where most people live.
- **The Golden Circle**, with geysers and a waterfall.
- **The Westfjords**, a remote fjord-cut peninsula.
- **The highlands**, a barren volcanic interior.
