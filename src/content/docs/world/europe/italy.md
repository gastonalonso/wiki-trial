**Italy** occupies a long peninsula reaching into the Mediterranean Sea, together with the large islands of Sicily and Sardinia. It is a country of mountains and hills, and has a rich urban history and some of the world's best-known cultural cities.

## Geography

The Alps form the northern border, with lakes such as Garda and Como lying at their foot. South of them the Po Valley is a broad, fertile [plain](../../../physical/landforms/plains/) and the country's main farming and industrial region. The Apennine [mountains](../../../physical/landforms/mountains/) run down the length of the peninsula like a spine. Italy has several active [volcanoes](../../../physical/landforms/volcanoes/): Vesuvius near Naples, Etna in Sicily and Stromboli. The [coastline](../../../physical/landforms/coastlines/) is long, with many bays and islands.

## Climate

Most of the country has a [Mediterranean](../../../physical/biomes/mediterranean/) climate, with hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. The Po Valley is more continental, with foggy, cold winters and humid summers, and the Alps are cold and snowy.

:::caution
Italy is seismically active, with earthquakes in the Apennines and volcanic hazards around Naples and Sicily.
:::

## Regions and places

- **The north**, with Milan, Turin and Venice.
- **Central Italy**, including Tuscany and Rome.
- **The south**, around Naples and the Amalfi coast.
- **Sicily and Sardinia**, the two major [islands](../../../physical/landforms/islands/).
