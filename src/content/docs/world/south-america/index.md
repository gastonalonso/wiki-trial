**South America** is the fourth-largest continent, about 17.8 million square kilometres, and lies mostly in the Southern Hemisphere. It is joined to North America by the Isthmus of Panama and is bounded by the Pacific and Atlantic oceans and the Caribbean Sea.

Its physical geography is organised around two features. The **Andes**, the longest continental mountain range on Earth, run down the whole western side, carrying volcanoes, high plateaus and the highest peak outside Asia. East of the Andes lie the Amazon basin, the largest river system by volume, the Brazilian Highlands, the Gran Chaco and the temperate pampas, finishing in the cold steppe of Patagonia.

The continent holds the largest tropical rainforest, one of the driest deserts, the Atacama, and some of the world's tallest waterfalls and largest glaciers outside the polar regions. Its climates range from equatorial to subpolar, and the cold Humboldt Current along the Pacific coast shapes the dry western coast and rich fisheries.

The pages in this section cover four countries. Related reading includes [mountains](../../physical/landforms/mountains/), [rivers](../../physical/water/rivers/) and the [Amazon rainforest](../../places/amazon-rainforest/).

## Countries

- [Argentina](argentina/): the Andes, the pampas and Patagonia in the far south.
- [Brazil](brazil/): the largest country on the continent, home to most of the Amazon.
- [Chile](chile/): a long, narrow country between the Andes and the Pacific.
- [Peru](peru/): a country of coastal desert, high Andes and Amazon lowlands.
