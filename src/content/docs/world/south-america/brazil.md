**Brazil** covers nearly half of South America and borders every other country on the continent except Chile and Ecuador. It is the fifth-largest country in the world by area and the only one on the continent where Portuguese is the national language, a legacy of its colonisation by Portugal.

## Geography

The north is dominated by the **Amazon basin**, whose river carries more water than any other on Earth ([rivers](../../../physical/water/rivers/)). The rainforest around it is the largest in the world, and most of it lies within Brazil; see [Amazon rainforest](../../../places/amazon-rainforest/) and [tropical rainforest](../../../physical/biomes/tropical-rainforest/). South of the basin the land rises to the Brazilian Highlands, a broad, old plateau with low mountains ([plateaus](../../../physical/landforms/plateaus/)). The Pantanal, in the southwest, is among the world's largest tropical [wetlands](../../../physical/water/wetlands/).

The Atlantic coastline is more than 7,000 kilometres long, with beaches, bays and the remnants of the Atlantic Forest. In the south, on the border with Argentina, are the Iguazu Falls ([waterfalls](../../../physical/water/waterfalls/)).

## Climate and biomes

Most of the country is tropical. The Amazon is hot and wet all year, while the central plateau has a distinct wet and dry season and holds the **cerrado**, a large [savanna](../../../physical/biomes/savanna/). The northeast interior includes the semi-arid *caatinga*, and the far south is subtropical with occasional frost. See [climate zones](../../../physical/climate/climate-zones/).

:::caution
Clearing for farms and pasture has removed a significant part of the Amazon forest in recent decades, which affects rainfall, wildlife and the global carbon balance. See [climate change](../../../physical/climate/climate-change/).
:::

## Regions and cities

Brazil is usually divided into five regions: North, Northeast, Southeast, South and Centre-West. São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro are among the largest cities in the Americas ([cities](../../../human/cities/)). The purpose-built capital, Brasília, was inaugurated in 1960 and lies on the central plateau.
