**Chile** is a long, narrow country on the western edge of South America. It extends about 4,300 kilometres from north to south yet is on average less than 200 kilometres wide, squeezed between the Pacific Ocean and the Andes. Its shape gives it an unusually wide range of climates.

## Geography

The Andes form the eastern border, and Chile has dozens of active [volcanoes](../../../physical/landforms/volcanoes/), reflecting its position along the Pacific "Ring of Fire", where the Nazca plate slides beneath South America. The country is among the most seismically active on Earth and has recorded the strongest earthquake ever measured, in 1960. Between the Andes and a lower coastal range lies the fertile Central Valley, where most people live.

The south is a maze of islands, channels and fjords with large icefields ([coastlines](../../../physical/landforms/coastlines/), [glaciers](../../../physical/landforms/glaciers/)). Chile also administers Easter Island, far out in the Pacific.

## Climate and biomes

The far north holds the **Atacama Desert**, one of the driest places on Earth, where some weather stations have gone years without measurable rain ([deserts](../../../physical/landforms/deserts/)). The central region has a [Mediterranean](../../../physical/biomes/mediterranean/) climate with dry summers and mild, wet winters, and supports vineyards and orchards. Farther south the climate turns cool and rainy, with [temperate forests](../../../physical/biomes/temperate-forests/), and Patagonia is cold and windswept. The cold Humboldt Current, part of the [ocean currents](../../../physical/climate/ocean-currents/), cools the coast and feeds rich fisheries.

:::note
Dryness in the Atacama is caused by a rain shadow from the Andes combined with cold coastal water that suppresses rainfall.
:::

## Regions

Santiago is the political and economic centre. Valparaíso is the main port, and the desert north is a mining region, notably for copper, of which Chile is the world's largest producer. The deep south is thinly populated and is reached mainly by sea or air.
