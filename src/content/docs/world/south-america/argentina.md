**Argentina** occupies most of the southern part of South America east of the Andes. It is the second-largest country on the continent and the eighth-largest in the world, and it extends from the tropics in the north to near the southern tip of the continent, giving it a wide range of landscapes.

## Geography

The western border follows the crest of the Andes, where **Aconcagua** is the highest mountain in both the Southern and Western hemispheres. See [mountains](../../../physical/landforms/mountains/). East of the mountains the **pampas**, a vast and fertile plain, are the agricultural heart of the country and the home of its cattle and grain industries ([plains](../../../physical/landforms/plains/), [grasslands](../../../physical/biomes/grasslands/)). North of the pampas is the hot, low Gran Chaco, and in the far south lies Patagonia, a cold, windy steppe that reaches the Atlantic in high cliffs.

The Paraná and Uruguay rivers join to form the Río de la Plata estuary at Buenos Aires ([estuaries](../../../physical/water/estuaries/), [rivers](../../../physical/water/rivers/)). In the northeast, the Iguazu River drops over the famous Iguazu Falls on the border with Brazil ([waterfalls](../../../physical/water/waterfalls/)).

## Climate and biomes

The north is subtropical and humid, the centre temperate, and Patagonia cold and dry because the Andes block moist westerly winds. The western Andean foothills of Mendoza are arid, which is why vineyards there rely on irrigation from mountain meltwater. The southern Andes carry large icefields; the Perito Moreno Glacier in Los Glaciares National Park is a well-known tidewater glacier ([glaciers](../../../physical/landforms/glaciers/)).

:::tip[Did you know?]
Aconcagua, at about 6,961 metres, is the highest mountain outside Asia.
:::

## Regions and cities

Buenos Aires and its surrounding province hold a third or more of the population, and the capital region is one of the largest urban areas in the Americas ([cities](../../../human/cities/)). The country shares long borders with Chile, Bolivia, Paraguay, Brazil and Uruguay ([borders](../../../human/borders/)), and the southern tip of the continent is shared with Chile on the island of Tierra del Fuego.
