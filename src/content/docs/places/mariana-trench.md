The **Mariana Trench** is a curved trench in the western Pacific Ocean, east of the Mariana Islands. It reaches a depth of nearly 11 kilometres at a spot called the Challenger Deep, deeper than Mount Everest is high. The trench formed where one tectonic plate sinks beneath another, and the pressure at the bottom is more than a thousand times that at the surface.
