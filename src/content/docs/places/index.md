Some places are so large, so old or so striking that they appear in almost every account of the planet. This section collects a dozen of them. Each article describes where the place is, how it formed, what lives there and how people use or protect it, and links back to the general pages on landforms, water, climate and biomes.

The places range from the highest summit on Earth to the deepest known point of the ocean floor, and from the driest deserts to the world's largest coral reef system. Several cross national borders, so they also show how physical features and [borders](../human/borders/) interact.

| Place | Country or countries | Kind of place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mount Everest | Nepal, China | Mountain |
| Sahara | Egypt, Morocco and about ten other countries | Hot desert |
| Amazon rainforest | Brazil, Peru and other countries | Tropical rainforest |
| Nile | Egypt, Ethiopia, Kenya and others | River |
| Grand Canyon | United States | Canyon |
| Great Barrier Reef | Australia | Coral reef |
| Lake Baikal | Russia | Rift lake |
| Dead Sea | Israel, Jordan, West Bank | Salt lake |
| Mariana Trench | Western Pacific Ocean | Ocean trench |
| Victoria Falls | Zambia, Zimbabwe | Waterfall |
| Ha Long Bay | Vietnam | Bay with karst islands |
| Uluru | Australia | Sandstone monolith |

## All places

- [Mount Everest](mount-everest/): the highest mountain above sea level, on the Nepal-China border.
- [Sahara](sahara/): the largest hot desert, crossing North Africa.
- [Amazon rainforest](amazon-rainforest/): the largest tropical forest, drained by the Amazon River.
- [Nile](nile/): the long river that carries water from East Africa to the Mediterranean.
- [Grand Canyon](grand-canyon/): a deep gorge cut by the Colorado River in Arizona.
- [Great Barrier Reef](great-barrier-reef/): a vast chain of coral reefs off Queensland.
- [Lake Baikal](lake-baikal/): the deepest lake on Earth, in Siberia.
- [Dead Sea](dead-sea/): a very salty lake at the lowest land surface on Earth.
- [Mariana Trench](mariana-trench/): the deepest part of the world's oceans.
- [Victoria Falls](victoria-falls/): a wide waterfall on the Zambezi River.
- [Ha Long Bay](ha-long-bay/): a bay filled with limestone islands in northern Vietnam.
- [Uluru](uluru/): a huge sandstone rock in central Australia.
