title: Time zones
description: Time zones divide the world into bands that share a standard clock time, set by offsets from Coordinated Universal Time.

A **time zone** is a region that observes the same standard time. Since the Sun appears highest at different moments in different places, local solar time varies continuously with longitude. Standard time zones group these into bands, so that people within one area can share the same clock.

## How zones are defined

The Earth turns through 360 degrees in 24 hours, or 15 degrees of [longitude](../latitude-and-longitude/) per hour. In theory, then, the world divides into 24 bands, each 15 degrees wide. Zones are numbered by their offset from **Coordinated Universal Time** (UTC), the successor of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), which is based on the prime meridian at Greenwich.

In practice, zone boundaries follow borders and local preference rather than meridians. Some zones differ from UTC by half or quarter hours: India uses UTC+5:30 and Nepal UTC+5:45 ([India](../../world/asia/india/), [Nepal](../../world/asia/nepal/)).

## The International Date Line

The date changes at an irregular line near the 180th meridian. Travelling west across it adds a day, and travelling east removes one. The line bends around island groups so that nearby places share a date. Fiji, for example, lies on the 180th meridian but keeps a single date throughout ([Fiji](../../world/oceania/fiji/)).

## Examples

| Place | Standard offset from UTC |
| --- | --- |
| Reykjavik, Iceland | UTC+0 |
| Paris, France | UTC+1 |
| Cairo, Egypt | UTC+2 |
| Tokyo, Japan | UTC+9 |
| Wellington, New Zealand | UTC+12 |
| New York, United States | UTC-5 |

Some countries cross several zones: the United States and [Canada](../../world/north-america/canada/) have six or more, and Russia has eleven. China, though wide enough for five, officially uses one zone for the whole country.[^1] Many regions also move their clocks forward in summer for daylight saving time, although the rule is far from universal.

:::note
Offsets listed above are for standard time, not for the summer period when daylight saving applies.
:::
