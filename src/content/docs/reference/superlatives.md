This page gathers the best-known world records. Figures are rounded, and some, such as river lengths and desert areas, depend on definitions and measurement methods.[^1] Records that have their own page in the wiki are linked.

## Highest mountain on each continent

| Continent | Mountain | Height (approx.) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asia | [Mount Everest](../../places/mount-everest/) | 8,849 m |
| South America | Aconcagua ([Argentina](../../world/south-america/argentina/)) | 6,961 m |
| North America | Denali ([United States](../../world/north-america/united-states/)) | 6,190 m |
| Africa | Kilimanjaro (Tanzania) | 5,895 m |
| Europe | Mount Elbrus (Russia) | 5,642 m |
| Antarctica | Vinson Massif ([Antarctica](../../world/antarctica/)) | 4,892 m |
| Oceania | Puncak Jaya (Indonesia) | 4,884 m |

Mainland Australia's highest peak, Mount Kosciuszko, is only about 2,228 metres ([Australia](../../world/oceania/australia/)). See also [mountains](../../physical/landforms/mountains/).

## Longest rivers

| River | Length (approx.) | Continent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Nile](../../places/nile/) | 6,650 km | Africa |
| Amazon | 6,400 km | South America |
| Yangtze | 6,300 km | Asia |
| Mississippi–Missouri | 6,275 km | North America |
| Yenisei–Angara | 5,500 km | Asia |

The Nile and the Amazon trade first place depending on where the source is drawn. By volume of water, the Amazon is far larger than any other river. See [rivers](../../physical/water/rivers/) and the [Amazon rainforest](../../places/amazon-rainforest/).

## Largest lakes

| Lake | Area (approx.) | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Caspian Sea | 371,000 km² | Between Europe and Asia |
| Lake Superior | 82,000 km² | Canada and United States |
| Lake Victoria | 68,800 km² | Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania |
| Lake Huron | 59,600 km² | Canada and United States |
| Lake Michigan | 58,000 km² | United States |

[Lake Baikal](../../places/lake-baikal/) in Russia, at about 31,500 km², is the deepest and oldest lake, reaching more than 1,600 metres. See [lakes](../../physical/water/lakes/).

## Largest deserts

| Desert | Area (approx.) | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Antarctic Desert | 14.2 million km² | Polar |
| Arctic Desert | 13.9 million km² | Polar |
| [Sahara](../../places/sahara/) | 9.2 million km² | Hot |
| Arabian Desert | 2.3 million km² | Hot |
| Gobi | 1.3 million km² | Cold |

Deserts are defined by low precipitation, not heat, so the polar regions count. See [deserts](../../physical/landforms/deserts/).

## Deepest points

| Feature | Depth or elevation (approx.) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Challenger Deep, [Mariana Trench](../../places/mariana-trench/) | about 10,935 m below sea level | Deepest known point in the oceans |
| Lake Baikal | about 1,640 m deep | Deepest lake |
| [Dead Sea](../../places/dead-sea/) shore | about 430 m below sea level | Lowest land elevation |

:::note
The Dead Sea's surface has been falling by roughly a metre a year in recent decades, so its elevation keeps changing.
:::
