A lake is a body of water that sits in a basin, surrounded by land. Most lakes hold fresh water, fed by rain, melting snow and [rivers](../rivers/).

## How lakes form

Glaciers, volcanoes and shifting ground all carve out basins that slowly fill with water. Many lakes in the north are only about ten thousand years old, left behind when the last ice sheets melted.
