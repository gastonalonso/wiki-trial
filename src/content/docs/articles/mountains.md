A mountain is a landform that rises well above the land around it. Mountains shape the weather nearby, and their snow feeds many [rivers](../rivers/).

## How mountains form

Most mountain ranges form where two plates of the Earth's crust push into each other and fold the rock upward. Others are volcanoes, built up from layers of cooled lava.
