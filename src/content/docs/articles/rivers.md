A river is water flowing downhill in a channel. It starts at a source, often a spring or melting snow in the [mountains](../mountains/), and ends at a mouth, where it meets the sea or a [lake](../lakes/).

## Parts of a river

- **Source:** where the river begins.
- **Tributaries:** smaller streams that join it along the way.
- **Mouth:** where the river ends.
