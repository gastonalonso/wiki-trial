**Migration** is the movement of people to live in a new place, either within a country or across [borders](../borders/). People move for work and education, to join family, or to escape conflict, persecution or hardship, and some are displaced by drought, floods or other effects of [climate change](../../physical/climate/climate-change/). The United Nations estimates that about 280 million people live outside their country of birth, around 3.5 per cent of the world.
